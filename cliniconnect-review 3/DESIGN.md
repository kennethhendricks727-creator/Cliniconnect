# CliniConnect Training OS

**Intent:** a private, calm learning desk for a dental-product representative. The visual signature is a thin emerald "care path" that links progress, lesson chapters and the product workflow without pretending the proposed product is live.

## Tokens
- Canvas `#101413`; surface `#171d1b`; raised `#1d2522`; line `#2c3934`
- Ink `#edf3ef`; muted `#9aaca3`; emerald `#5ee3a3`; amber `#f3c871`
- Typography: Inter/system sans; compact, editorial headings; mono only for lesson codes.
- Radius 14px for surfaces, 9px for controls. Shadows are absent; depth comes from borders and contrast.

## Behavior
Two primary destinations only: Dashboard and Training. All completion actions give immediate visible confirmation and persist locally. Proposed capability statements always carry an explicit readiness label.
